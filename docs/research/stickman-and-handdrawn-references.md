# Stickman and hand-drawn references (owner links, 2026-10-15)

Owner review of 0.28: the drawing hand belongs to the **stickman** engine (an option: the hand draws the scene, or the plain stickman), the hand must look and move better, the stickman engine gets the styles of two reference videos, and the **hand-drawn** engine must be its own style, modelled on a third reference. Every engine keeps its own visuals and its own editing. Frames were sampled in the browser (no downloads); we learn techniques, never copy drawings, wording or identity (30/70 rule).

## 1. Stickman reference A - "POV: You're Every Rank In The Norteños" (The Life Outline, 9:35)

- **Look:** warm paper palette (cream #F4E9DA, sand, olive, maroon, slate), soft brown outlines, flat colour with light texture; fully illustrated places (streets, brick walls, rooms, a liquor-store shelf, a dumpster corner).
- **Characters:** big round white heads with tiny dot eyes and short brow lines (deadpan expressions); filled rounded torsos in muted shirt colours ("bean bodies"); thin stick arms and legs; colour marks a group (red for one side).
- **Editing:** "Level 1: The Runner"-style chapter cards (white words on dark navy, fade in and out) open each rank; hard cuts every 1.5-3 s; cinematic framing - wide establishing shot, over-the-shoulder (a big figure cut by the frame edge, a small group behind), object inserts with nobody in them (a bandana on a fence, graffiti, cash on a table), a top-down table; subtle living motion (walk cycles, a head turn) and a still camera.
- **Story:** a progression ladder (rank by rank), each step a short arc: the role, what it asks, what it costs.

## 2. Stickman reference B - "What Did Ancient Humans Actually Do All Year?" (Zenn, 11:13)

- **Look:** white page, thick black marker lines with uneven weight, bright flat fills (yellow sun, blue water, green grass strip as the ground), crude-on-purpose doodle drawing.
- **Characters:** classic stick figures with a circle head, very big round eyes with pupils, messy scribbled hair, sweat drops and shaking lines for emotion; objects with faces (an angry sun).
- **Editing:** visual gags for every line (carrying a pond on the head to explain water), extreme close-ups of a face for reactions, diagrams drawn the same way (a season wheel), cuts every 1-1.5 s, small loops of motion (sweat drops, a mouth), no camera moves.

## 3. Hand-drawn reference - "The Economics of Owning a Cargo Fleet" (Mr. Finance, 22:34)

The channel (80.5K subscribers, 122 videos) runs a 20-40 minute series, "The Economics of Owning a ___"; its most-watched (Gym, 308K) uses an older look (stick figures on a pastel wall, one big hand-lettered keyword per shot: DREAM, BUILD, PLAN, LIE, TRUTH). The linked video is the new look:

- **Look:** white paper; illustrated objects with clean black ink outlines, flat colour and light coloured-pencil shading; cute objects with faces (trucks that smile); people drawn as cartoon people (suits, caps, beards), not stick figures; almost no backgrounds (a ground line, a road, a cut-away room).
- **Annotations do the explaining:** handwritten capital labels with curved arrows ("8,000 MILES", "1,200 GALLONS"), sticky notes in yellow, green and blue, price tags on a string ("$170,000 - $250,000"), an arrow-shaped callout tag ("$3,000 - $6,000"), a clipboard checklist held by a hand, dashed connector lines between things, bold arrows pointing down, a hand-drawn bar chart ("UP 9%"), dollar signs above things, money stacks, a hand from the edge handing over cash.
- **Editing:** one idea per illustration, held 2-5 s, hard cuts; elements and money move a little (cash flying behind a truck, a hand pushing money in); diagrams build the comparison.
